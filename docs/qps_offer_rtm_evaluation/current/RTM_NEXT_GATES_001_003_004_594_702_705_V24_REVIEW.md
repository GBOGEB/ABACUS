## Canonical item review

### RTM-001 — complete contractual responsibility

**Anchor:** PDF p24, §4.1 General Requirements.

The Contractor is fully responsible for ensuring Deliverables and Services meet the Contract requirements. The canonical requirement further prevents reliance on alleged lack/insufficiency of information after offer submission/question deadlines and states that missing information during performance is not grounds for a Variation.

**OFFER boundary:** OFFER-01 primary; OFFER-02/03 supporting/shared-section.

**Review focus:** completeness of scope assumptions; explicit information-gap register; prevention of hidden exclusions; traceability between tender clarifications, accepted assumptions and execution baseline; no automatic variation entitlement from information gaps.

### RTM-003 — referenced standards documents

**Anchor:** PDF p24, §4.1 General Requirements.

The Contractor is responsible for obtaining the corresponding documents for referenced norms and standards.

**OFFER boundary:** OFFER-01 primary; OFFER-02/03 supporting/shared-section.

**Review focus:** controlled standards register; edition/revision applicability; access to normative documents; treatment of superseded standards and approved equivalents; linkage to design, procurement, verification and conformity evidence.

### RTM-004 — deviations and modifications

**Anchor:** PDF p25, §4.1 General Requirements.

Any deviation or modification requires prior written SCK CEN approval before implementation. Tender-stage deviations must be explicitly identified in the required dedicated offer section; acceptance of the offer does not silently accept unidentified deviations. Unidentified deviations become Contractor-responsibility Non-Conformities and do not constitute grounds for a Variation.

**OFFER boundary:** OFFER-02 primary; OFFER-01/03 supporting/shared-section.

**Review focus:** one controlled deviation/assumption/addition register; explicit disposition and approval evidence; prevention of technical documents silently introducing new deviations; configuration control after approval; reconciliation of negotiation outcomes with contract baseline.

### RTM-594 — equipment-damage prevention, detection and mitigation

**Anchor:** PDF p116, §5 Safety and Protection Requirements.

The QPS shall be designed to prevent, detect and mitigate conditions that could damage the QPS, so far as reasonably practicable.

**OFFER boundary:** no OFFER link expected.

**Review focus:** equipment-protection hazard register distinct from personnel-safety analysis; detection coverage; automatic/manual mitigation; fail-safe states; recovery consequences; links to alarms/interlocks, abnormal-event SAT tests and maintenance diagnostics.
