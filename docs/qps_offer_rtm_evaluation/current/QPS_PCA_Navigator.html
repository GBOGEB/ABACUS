QPS PCA Navigator — component structure of the RTM/OFFER scoring dimensions

Principal Component Analysis over the 7 raw 0–3 dimension scores (Safety/Legal, Reliability, Performance, Functional, Quality, Lifecycle, Cost) across 722 RTM items, from current/pca_results_v23.json and pca_adequacy_check.json. Built as the visual counterpart to QPS_RTM_BT_Navigator_v22.html — this thread previously had no visual presentation, only raw JSON. Not stale against the current v24 SSOT — the "v23" in the filename is the intermediate workbook version PCA was computed against, verified 2026-09-02 by a direct row-level diff: RTM_RANKING (725 rows) and OFFER_RANKING (53 rows) — the only sheets that feed these 7 dimension scores — are byte-identical between QPS_OFFER_Evaluation_FULL_v23.xlsx and _v24.xlsx; v24 only adds two unrelated sheets (PCA_ANALYSIS, OFFER_PHASE_EXPANSION) on top.

Variance explained

How much of the total spread across items is captured by each component. The first four components together explain 73.2% — after that, returns drop off quickly.

Sampling adequacy (KMO)

Kaiser–Meyer–Olkin measure — whether there's enough shared structure across items for PCA to be meaningful. Both figures are below the commonly-cited 0.6 minimum-usable threshold; disclosed here rather than omitted.

0.656
⚠ mediocre

Item-level (N=722, p=7, N/p=103.1) — clears the 10× sample-size rule of thumb despite the mediocre adequacy band.

0.576
⚠ miserable

Domain-level (N=22, p=7, N/p=3.1) — fails both the 5× and 10× sample-size rules; the domain-level PCA reading should be treated as illustrative only, not confirmatory.

Kaiser 1974 KMO scale, cited directly in the source JSON as "a widely-cited rule of thumb, not a universal law."

Component loadings

How strongly each of the 7 scoring dimensions pulls on the first 3 components. Blue = positive loading, red = negative, white = no relationship. PC1 reads as a Safety/Quality/Lifecycle axis pulling against Performance/Functional; PC2 is dominated by Reliability; PC3 by Cost. Click a PC column header to sort dimensions by strength of pull on that component; click again to reset to the original L/R/P/F/Q/LC/C order.

Domain structure (PC1 × PC2)

All 22 requirement domains positioned by their mean PC1/PC2 score; bubble size = item count. Domains higher/right load more on the Safety-Quality-Lifecycle axis; domains lower/left load more on Performance-Functional. Hover any bubble for its exact values.

Domain sizes

The same 22 domains by item count alone — 3 to 202 items, a 67× range where a linear scale compresses every domain below ~30 items into an unreadable sliver. Toggle to log scale to compare the smaller domains meaningfully.

Bulk vs. distinctive items

Of the 722 RTM items, 174 (24%) sit far enough from the PC1/PC2 population center to be considered distinctive rather than typical — these are the items whose scoring profile most differs from the bulk of the requirement set.
