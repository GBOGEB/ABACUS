# Intent Manifest

## Primary intent

Create a traceable, reduced modelling package that answers the Applicant question on Line S pressure build-up and helium recovery flow acceptance for RTM-261 and RTM-292.

## Non-goals for current wave

- Do not reproduce full SIMCRYOGENICS.
- Do not finalize FMECA.
- Do not freeze valve states before Appendix 8.4 extraction.
- Do not claim final Line S allowable pressure until design pressure, relief set pressure, compressor suction limits, and effective volume are confirmed.

## Immediate outputs

- Applicant-facing technical answer.
- Reduced Line S pressure-buffer model.
- Corrected heat-load sensitivity.
- Source register.
- Scenario matrix.
- RTM traceability.
- Index and glossary artifacts.
- Parallel helper issues for coding agents.

## Decision principle

Line S may be credited as a bounded transient buffer only if the pressure remains below the most restrictive applicable limit and no credited no-loss case opens relief or vents helium.
