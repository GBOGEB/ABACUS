5. Pressure Drop — QRB to WCS

\[\Delta P = f \cdot \frac{L}{D} \cdot \frac{\rho v^2}{2} = 0.0139 \times \frac{50}{0.15} \times \frac{2.19 \times 9.62^2}{2} = 501 \text{ Pa} = 5.0 \text{ mbar}\]
