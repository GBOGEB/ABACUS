⚡ CRITICAL CONSTRAINT: 400V 3-Phase AC Supply
All equipment MUST be specified for 400V 3-phase 50 Hz AC with variable frequency drive (VFD) capability (50–72 Hz). No 690V or 11 kV alternatives without explicit approval and step-up transformer design.

1. Project Overview

The MYRRHA (MYRRHA is a research reactor facility) Helium Conditioning Circuit requires high-capacity compressed air recovery and processing to achieve 350 g/s helium flow at 300 °C and 20 bar outlet pressure. The HCC shall consist of three (3) identical oil-free screw compressors (LP→HP stages) with integrated variable frequency drives (VFDs), soft-start contactor, cooling systems, and pressure control instrumentation.

Primary objective: Supply three (3) FSD-series screw compressors (oil-free, preferred) capable of delivering 316 g/s helium flow at approximately 63–68 Hz operating frequency. Redundancy: N+2 (any two units can sustain 50% flow).

2.3 Operating Conditions

  • Duty cycle: Continuous operation, 24/7 (some turndown scenarios possible; see §2.4)
  • Ambient air inlet: 15–25 °C, ≤70% relative humidity (IP54 air filters; assumes climate-controlled room)
  • Coolant inlet (HX1, HX3): Circulating chiller: 12 °C, ~0.5 m³/min flow per unit
  • Elevation: ~200 m above sea level (minimal air density correction; ≈2% loss)
  • Vibration limits: ISO 10816-3 Group 1 (<7.1 mm/s RMS velocity at 10–200 Hz)
  • Noise limit: ≤85 dB(A) @ 1 m (oil-free compressors typically 78–82 dB; confirms requirement)

4.4 Electrical Safety & Protection

  • Motor protection (thermal): Embedded PTC thermistor or RTD sensor; cutout at 155 °C winding temp
  • Pressure relief (safety): Set @ 17 bar; manual bleed valve for service
  • Check valve (non-return): Prevents backflow if motor stalls; cracking pressure 0.5 bar above discharge
  • Solenoid isolation valve: Pilot-operated, 24 VDC coil; manual override for emergency venting
  • Soft-starter contactor: 3-phase soft-start unit; limits inrush to ≤3× rated current (coordinate with site electrical designer)

5.3 Coolant Circulation

  • Source: Central chiller (site-supplied), 12 °C setpoint, 0.5–1.0 m³/min per unit
  • Piping: 1.0″ (25 mm) stainless steel or aluminium alloy; insulated to prevent condensation
  • Temperature sensor: Outlet thermistor (4–20 mA output); alarm if >50 °C (cooler fouling indicator)
  • Flow switch: Normally-open switch @ inlet; closes if flow drops below 0.3 m³/min (pump failure detection)
  • Isolation valves: Ball valves (manual) on inlet/outlet per cooler for service isolation

6.2 Control Interface

  • Frequency command: 0–10 VDC input (0 Hz = 0V, 72 Hz = 10V); site PLC provides ramp
  • Status feedback: Modbus RTU gateway (optional); allows remote diagnostics
  • Interlocks:
    • Coolant inlet flow switch → must close before motor start (prevents thermal overshoot)
    • Motor overtemp → soft-stop command (ramp down over 30 s)
    • Discharge overpressure (>18 bar) → proportional relief opens automatically
    • Any safety trip → solenoid isolation valve closes (vents gas to safe exhaust)

8. Delivery & Installation

8.1 Scope of Supply (Kaeser)

  • Three (3) complete FSD 575 SFC packages: compressor + motor + VFD (if integrated) + cooler + controls + pressure relief
  • Soft-start contractor (if not Kaeser-integrated, separate quotation)
  • Vibration isolators + base frame for each unit
  • High-pressure hose assemblies (discharge: compressor → manifold/cooler)
  • Low-pressure hose assemblies (suction recovery: inlet → compressor)
  • Coolant hoses + fittings (from chiller to cooler)
  • Electrical: Power cable terminations, control wiring harnesses, 24 VDC sensor wiring
  • Spare parts kit: Oil seals (if oil-flooded option), V-belts (if used), compressor valve plates
  • Technical documentation: Manuals, performance curves, wiring diagrams, parts lists, CAD drawings (3D STEP preferred)

8.2 Delivery & Site Preparation

  • Delivery location: MYRRHA site, Belgium (transport via EUR pallet)
  • Delivery schedule: Lead time 8–12 weeks from order (to be confirmed); expedite options available at cost
  • Packaging: Wooden skid with load straps; protective covers on all openings
  • Site infrastructure (owner-supplied): Concrete floor (≥300 mm, reinforced); 400V 3-phase power supply; coolant circulation pump + chiller

8.3 Commissioning & Testing

  • Factory acceptance test (FAT): Kaeser to perform idle run + pressure relief venting @ rated discharge pressure
  • Site acceptance test (SAT): Full load test @ 68 Hz; verify flow, pressure, temperature, motor current within ±5% of guarantee
  • Performance acceptance: Cooling water temperature rise matches ±5% of design; motor winding temp <145 °C (continuous)
