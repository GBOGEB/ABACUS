RCW Heat Exchanger Design

30 °C nominal inlet · ΔT 25 K · oil 85 °C → 30 °C precool
  • HCC CW split (per skid, FSD 575): 323.9 kW total → 197.6 kW oil + 126.3 kW sensible He (84 K → 300 K)
  • RCW per skid: 6.83 m³/h @ ΔT 25 K · plant 20.5 m³/h (3-skid) / 27.3 m³/h (4-skid)
  • HX strategy: 1 duty per skid + 1 shared plant standby (manifold N+1)
  • Loss-of-1 without standby ≈ −32 % He turn-down or +5 K oil-loop offset

WCS Energy Cascade — Waterfall View

Grid → motor → shaft → ideal isothermal → heat sinks (350 g/s total baseline)

  • Grid input: 1,290 kW (Kaeser HP 990 + PVPS 150 + HVAC 130 + Controls 20)
  • Compressor motors: 1,140 kW (HP 990 + PVPS 150)
  • Motor efficiency (η≈96.7%): 37.6 kW losses → cooling air / motor water
  • Shaft delivered: 1,102 kW to compression
  • W_iso (ideal isothermal): 551 kW (50% efficiency baseline)
  • Excess compression heat: 551 kW → cooling water (RCW + PCW)

Note: 350 g/s total flow split: 300 g/s HP (85.7%) + 50 g/s PVPS (14.3%)

Exergy Analysis — Second-Law Efficiency

Where the work goes (η_II = 44.0%)

  • Exergy in (electrical): 1 482 kW (pure exergy)
  • Useful exergy out (compressed He): 651.6 kW = 44.0%
  • Destruction: motors 44.5 kW · compression irreversibility 651.6 kW · HVAC 134.7 kW
  • Lever: multi-stage intercooled compression → η_II 55–60%
  • Waste-heat recovery: 100–300 kW available at 60–90 °C
