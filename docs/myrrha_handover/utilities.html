2 · PCW (Process Cooling Water)

Sensible heat balance on PCW:

$\dot Q = \dot m_\text{w}\, c_p\, \Delta T \;\;\Longrightarrow\;\; \dot V = \dfrac{3600\,\dot Q}{\rho\, c_p\, \Delta T}$

Per-skid heat from datasheet: ALaT 323.9 kW · LKT 230.9 kW. Per-skid CW: ALaT 18.2 m³/h · LKT 15.5 m³/h. Implied vendor ΔT (back-calc):

$\Delta T_\text{ALaT} = \dfrac{3600 \cdot 323.9}{995 \cdot 4.186 \cdot 18.2} \approx \mathbf{15.4~\text{K}}$
$\Delta T_\text{LKT} = \dfrac{3600 \cdot 230.9}{995 \cdot 4.186 \cdot 15.5} \approx \mathbf{12.9~\text{K}}$

4 · Sample calculation walk-through (ALaT @ 336 g/s)

Capacity check:

$\dot m_\text{per skid} = \dfrac{336}{3} = 112~\text{g/s}$   ≤   $112.54~\text{g/s}$ basis ⇒ OK
$f_\text{est} = 72 \cdot \dfrac{112}{112.54} \approx 71.65~\text{Hz}$

Plant utilities (linear scaling on package input):

$P_\text{plant} \approx 3 \cdot 348.54 \cdot \dfrac{112}{112.54} \approx \mathbf{1040.6~\text{kW}}$
$\dot Q_\text{PCW} \approx 3 \cdot 323.9 \cdot \dfrac{112}{112.54} \approx \mathbf{966.9~\text{kW}}$

Linear scaling is conservative for screw compressors at constant pressure ratio — actual specific-power degrades slightly off the design point.

5 · PVPS (WCS.LCC) — isothermal sizing at γ = 50 g/s

Status: v0.4 placeholder pending vendor confirmation. Sizing uses an isothermal compression baseline with ηiso = 0.50 and motor efficiency ηmot = 0.967 (Kaeser-class).

Helium ideal-gas isothermal work for $\dot m = 0.050$ kg/s, $T = 293$ K, $p_1 = 0.1$ barA, $p_2 = 1.05$ barA, $R_\text{He} = 2077$ J/(kg·K):

$\dot W_\text{iso} = \dot m\, R\, T\, \ln(p_2/p_1) = 0.050 \cdot 2077 \cdot 293 \cdot \ln(10.5) \approx \mathbf{71.55~\text{kW}}$
$\dot W_\text{shaft} = \dot W_\text{iso} / \eta_\text{iso} = 71.55 / 0.50 = \mathbf{143.1~\text{kW}}$
$P_\text{motor} = \dot W_\text{shaft} / \eta_\text{mot} = 143.1 / 0.967 \approx \mathbf{147.98~\text{kW}}$

Heat split: HVAC = 10 % of motor electrical, cooling water = 90 % of remaining heat.

$\dot Q_\text{HVAC} = 0.10 \cdot 147.98 \approx \mathbf{14.80~\text{kW}}$
$\dot Q_\text{CW} = 0.90 \cdot 147.98 \approx \mathbf{133.18~\text{kW}}$
$\dot V_\text{CW} = \dfrac{3600 \cdot 133.18}{995 \cdot 4.186 \cdot 15} \approx \mathbf{7.67~\text{m}^3/\text{h}}$  (at ΔT = 15 K)

§7 Thermodynamic Analysis — Energy and Exergy Balances

Full interactive charts on thermo-analysis.html. Calculation steps below.

7.1 Isothermal compression work

Reversible isothermal work on an ideal gas:

$$W_{iso} = \dot m\, R\, T_s\, \ln\!\left(\frac{P_2}{P_1}\right)$$

  • HCC, 350 g/s, 1.05→14 barA, T_s=308 K: W_iso = 0.350·2077.1·308·ln(13.33) = 580.0 kW
  • PVPS, 50 g/s, 0.10→1.05 barA, T_s=293 K: W_iso = 0.050·2077.1·293·ln(10.5) = 71.55 kW

7.2 Shaft and electrical demand (η_iso = 50%, η_motor = 96.7%)

$$W_{shaft} = \frac{W_{iso}}{\eta_{iso}}, \qquad P_{el} = \frac{W_{shaft}}{\eta_{motor}}$$

  • HCC shaft = 580/0.50 = 1 160 kW; HCC electrical = 1160/0.967 = 1 199.6 kW (3 motors)
  • PVPS shaft = 143.1 kW; PVPS electrical = 148.0 kW (10 motors)
  • HVAC (10% of motor electrical) = 134.7 kW → Grand total ≈ 1 482 kW

7.3 Polytropic / actual discharge from heat balance (FSD 575)

Given: motor 315 kW, shaft 304.6 kW, 112.5 g/s, cooling water 323.9 kW. With Wiso = 186.4 kW:

$$\eta_{iso,eff} = \frac{W_{iso}}{W_{shaft}} = \frac{186.4}{304.6} = 61.2\%$$

Excess heat dissipated = 304.6 − 186.4 = 118.2 kW. If all heated the gas adiabatically: $\Delta T = 118200/(0.1125·5193) = 202\,\text{K}$ → T_d ≈ 510 K. In practice oil-flooded screw → effective gas T after package ≈ 360–400 K (oil cooling intercepts ~60% of compression heat).

7.4 Exergy balance

For an ideal-gas stream relative to environment (T₀, P₀):

$$\dot E = \dot m\big[c_p(T-T_0) - T_0 c_p\ln(T/T_0) + R T_0 \ln(P/P_0)\big]$$

For isothermal compression (T = T₀): $\dot E_{out} = \dot m R T_0 \ln(P/P_0) = W_{iso}$.

  • Useful exergy delivered: 580 + 71.6 = 651.6 kW
  • Exergy in (electrical, pure exergy): 1 482 kW
  • Second-law efficiency: η_II = 651.6/1482 = 44.0%
  • Exergy destruction breakdown: motor irreversibility 44.5 kW, compression irreversibility 651.6 kW, HVAC 134.7 kW

7.5 Cooling-water heat split

Per HCC unit at 350/3 = 117 g/s: shaft 387 kW, excess heat 193 kW → ~60% to RCW oil (116 kW), ~40% to PCW gas sensible (77 kW).
