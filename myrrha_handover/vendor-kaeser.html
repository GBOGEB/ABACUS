90% Kaeser assumption · FSD 475 SFC / FSD 575 SFC · Corrected technical data · v0.4.5

⚠ 90% Kaeser Assumption: This analysis assumes Kaeser oil-free screw compressors (FSD series) for the LP→HP HCC skids. All cost figures are rough engineering estimates (±25%) pending formal quotation. Utility calculations are based on Kaeser published data sheets and standard engineering practice.
🔴 v0.4.5 Corrections (Critical):
  • PVPS flow accounting: Total flow = Kaeser HP + PVPS (PVPS is included in total, not additive). If total = 350 g/s and PVPS = 50 g/s, then Kaeser HP = 300 g/s.
  • FSD 575 @ 72 Hz = 112 g/s per unit (maximum frequency for 400V 3-phase).
  • FSD 575 @ 60 Hz ≈ 93 g/s per unit (interpolated from datasheet). Rated power @ 60 Hz = 315 kW.
  • Recommended: 3× FSD 575 @ ~63 Hz for 316 g/s actual need (minimal overcapacity).
  • Isothermal efficiency = 50% → Actual electrical = Isothermal / 0.5.
  • Oil-free primary recommendation (alternatives: oil-flooded direct, dry with interstage cooling).
  • VFD range: 50–72 Hz (400V 3-phase AC available).

⚡ Electrical Requirements (MT-13: 400V Power Analysis)

Critical Constraint: 400V 3-Phase AC Supply

Kaeser FSD motors MUST be specified as 400V 3-phase 50–72 Hz variable frequency drives (VFDs). No 690V or 11 kV alternatives without step-up transformers (not recommended for this application).

Vendor Engagement Checklist (MT-11)

Before releasing RFQ to Kaeser, confirm the following electrical requirements:

  • ✓ Confirm FSD 575 availability in 400V 3-phase 50–72 Hz: Is the motor winding rated for 400V AC with VFD duty? Confirm nameplate label.
  • ✓ Motor power vs. frequency curve: Provide motor power (kW) and compressor flow (g/s) at key frequencies: 50 Hz, 55 Hz, 60 Hz, 65 Hz, 68 Hz (design), 72 Hz (max).
  • ✓ VFD coupling (Kaeser-supplied vs. external): Will Kaeser provide integrated VFD cabinets, or should we source separate drives? (Preferred: integrated for warranty/support.)
  • ✓ Motor cooling requirements: Are motor cooling fans powered separately, or included in main motor current? (Impacts total electrical load estimate.)
  • ✓ Harmonic filter compatibility: Will Kaeser VFDs work with external active harmonic filter? (Some VFDs need special input filtering.)
  • ✓ Power factor target: Kaeser to confirm: Does their system achieve PF >0.95 with PFC capacitor bank installed?
  • ✓ Soft-starter requirement: Recommend: Soft-starter contactor on HCC main supply to limit inrush current (reduces grid stress).
