§1HCC Cooling-Water Split — Oil vs Sensible Helium

Per-skid total cooling-water heat rejection (ALaT FSD 575 SFC, vendor sheet): $\dot Q_\text{CW,tot} = 323.9~\text{kW}$ at $\dot m_\text{He} = 112.54$ g/s. The duty splits into the oil-cooler train (compressor oil 85 °C → 30 °C) and the sensible helium after-cooler (post oil-separator, returning compression-heated helium back to ambient for the isothermal design condition).

1.1 Sensible helium after-cooler

$\dot Q_\text{He} = \dot m_\text{He}\,c_{p,\text{He}}\,\Delta T$   with   $c_{p,\text{He}} = 5.193~\text{kJ/(kg·K)}$,   $\Delta T = T_\text{out}-T_\text{in}$

Using the isothermal-design boundary stated in the WCS interface (helium handled across $T_\text{in,He}=84~\text{K}$ → $T_\text{out,He}=300~\text{K}$ on the cooling-water side over the combined HX train, $\Delta T_\text{He}=216~\text{K}$):

$\dot Q_\text{He} = 0.11254 \cdot 5.193 \cdot 216 \approx \mathbf{126.3~\text{kW per skid}}$

1.2 Oil-cooler duty (by difference)

$\dot Q_\text{oil} = \dot Q_\text{CW,tot} - \dot Q_\text{He} = 323.9 - 126.3 \approx \mathbf{197.6~\text{kW per skid}}$

Oil-side energy balance, oil flow $\dot m_\text{oil}$, $c_{p,\text{oil}} \approx 2.10~\text{kJ/(kg·K)}$, $\Delta T_\text{oil} = 85-30 = 55~\text{K}$:

$\dot m_\text{oil} = \dfrac{\dot Q_\text{oil}}{c_{p,\text{oil}}\,\Delta T_\text{oil}} = \dfrac{197.6}{2.10 \cdot 55} \approx \mathbf{1.71~\text{kg/s}} \approx 6.2~\text{t/h}$  (≈ 110 L/min @ ρ ≈ 870 kg/m³)

§3N+1 & Redundancy Analysis

Strategy: one HX per skid in active duty + one shared plant-level standby HX on a manifold, isolatable on each skid via two motor-operated valves. This gives plant N+1 without doubling installed area.

3.2 Loss-of-unit math (3-skid case, no standby)

$\dot Q_\text{avail,2HX} = 2 \cdot 200 = 400~\text{kW}$   vs   required 593 kW ⇒ deficit 193 kW
$\dfrac{\Delta \dot m_\text{He}}{\dot m_\text{He}} \approx -\dfrac{\Delta \dot Q}{\dot Q} \approx -\dfrac{193}{593} \approx \mathbf{-32~\%}$   (oil load is dominant in $\dot Q_\text{oil}$ ∝ $\dot m_\text{He}$ at constant pressure ratio)

Mitigation: raise oil-loop set-point from 30 → 35 °C (+ 5 K hot-end), regaining ~20 % of the deficit; remaining ~12 % met by helium-flow turndown (≈ 308 g/s).

§4COMBI Unit Concept — Fixed-speed + VFD

The COMBI configuration pairs one fixed-speed FSD 575 SFC (locked at 64 Hz for HP-flow trim margin) with one VFD-equipped sister unit that floats 50–72 Hz to cover the operational band.

4.1 Linear scaling from the 72 Hz vendor anchor

$\dot m(f) = \dot m_{72} \cdot \dfrac{f}{72} \quad\text{with}\quad \dot m_{72} = 112.54~\text{g/s}$  (per-skid)
$P_\text{motor}(f) \approx P_{72} \cdot \dfrac{f}{72}, \quad \dot V_\text{CW}(f) \approx \dot V_{72} \cdot \dfrac{f}{72}$   with   $P_{72}=315~\text{kW},\;\dot V_{72}=18.2~\text{m³/h}$
