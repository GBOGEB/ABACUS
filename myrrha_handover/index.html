Single-package HTML ecosystem · cross-linked views · v0.4.5 · last touched

What this is

A self-contained engineering handover bundle for the Warm Compressor Station (WCS) of the MYRRHA cryogenic plant (QPLANT). It compares the two pre-studied warm-compressor concepts (Kaeser FSD 575 SFC as offered by ALaT vs Kaeser FSD 475 SFC as offered by LKT), checks the requested target mass-flow range against 3-skid capacity, and consolidates utility requirements at a 350 g/s @ 14 barG baseline.
