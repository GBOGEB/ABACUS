§4 Energy & Exergy Balance — Multi-Level Sunburst

Energy distribution (1st law)

Exergy distribution (2nd law, Ψ = electrical = 100%)

Exergy of electrical input: pure exergy, $\dot E_{el} = P_{el}$.
Exergy of compressed helium stream (relative to environment T₀, P₀):
$\dot E_{He} = \dot m\big[c_p(T - T_0) - T_0 c_p \ln(T/T_0) + R T_0 \ln(P/P_0)\big]$
For isothermal compression ($T = T_0$): $\dot E_{He} = \dot m R T_0 \ln(P/P_0) = W_{iso}$.
Second-law (exergetic) efficiency: $\eta_{II} = \dot E_{He,out}/\dot E_{el,in}$.
