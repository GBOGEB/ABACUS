System overview

The PVPS (Pumping / Vacuum Pre-compression Station) is the low-pressure side of the Warm Compressor Station (WCS.LCC). It handles helium return at sub-atmospheric pressure and lifts it to the HCC suction header at ~1.05 barA. Total target flow γ = 50 g/s distributed across 10 identical units (LP1…LP10) in a 2×5 layout with N+1 redundancy.

  • Baseline: 10 units × 5.0 g/s = 50 g/s (all online)
  • N+1 normal: 9 units × 5.56 g/s = 50 g/s (1 standby) — VFD-driven
  • Degraded: 8 units × 6.25 g/s = 50 g/s (1 maintenance + 1 standby)
