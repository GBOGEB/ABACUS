🎯 PVPS Primary Role: Inventory Control & Redundancy
The Pre-Vacuum Pumping System evacuates helium from the cryogenic coldbox during startup and manages helium inventory during operation. PVPS does NOT supply the main 300 g/s flow (HCC does). PVPS reduces coldbox pressure to maintain cryogenic conditions and provides redundant evacuation during thermal transients.

1.2 Helium Inventory Flow Accounting

⚠ Critical model correction (v0.4.5 final):
Total helium flow to coldbox = 350 g/s
• HCC supplies: 300 g/s @ 20 bar, 300 °C (main cryogenic loop)
• PVPS removes: 50 g/s @ variable pressure (inventory control)
• Net result: 300 g/s flows through coldbox; 50 g/s exits via PVPS

Old (WRONG) model: Total = Kaeser + PVPS (additive). NEW (CORRECT): PVPS is parasitic draw from total inlet.

2.2 Compression Ratio & Pump Sizing

Target inlet pressure: 0.1–0.5 barA (coldbox suction)
Discharge pressure: Atmospheric (stack or vent) ~1.0 barA
Compression ratio per unit: P_discharge / P_inlet ≈ 10 (worst case: 1.0 / 0.1)

Implication: PVPS units must be designed for vacuum-to-atmospheric compression (turbo or rotary screw suitable).

Four (4) mainstream options for vacuum-to-atmospheric helium pumping:

3.2 Recommended Approach

Primary recommendation: 10× rotary screw (dry, oil-free) pump units
• Parallel piping configuration (9 active + 1 standby for N+1 redundancy)
• Individual 24 VDC solenoid isolation valve per pump
• Common discharge manifold (with check valve per pump to prevent backflow)
• Single relief valve (atmospheric setpoint ~1.0 barA + 0.1 bar margin)

Fallback: If screw not available, consider 10× rotary vane 2-stage units (each ~25 kW, slightly higher power budget).

4.3 Control & Protection

  • Start method: Direct on-line (DOL) or soft-start (recommended to reduce inrush)
  • Speed control (if not constant): VFD (0–10 VDC command) or fixed speed contactor
  • Motor protection: Thermal overload relay (Class 10); embedded PTC thermistor (optional, if VFD)
  • Pressure protection: Discharge relief valve (solenoid-piloted, atmospheric setpoint ~1.0 barA)
  • Solenoid isolation valve: 24 VDC coil; pilot-operated; fail-safe (closes on de-energise)
  • Check valve: Inlet (suction) to prevent backflow during shutdown
  • Non-return valve: Discharge side per pump (prevents reverse flow from common manifold)

5.3 Piping & Connections

  • Suction line (coldbox → pump): 1.5″–2″ stainless steel or aluminium tube; insulated (prevent condensation)
  • Discharge line (pump → manifold): 1″ stainless; carries atmospheric helium; low pressure drop target <0.1 bar
  • Vent stack (manifold → atmosphere): 1.5″ duct; thermal insulation if discharge temp >40 °C (helium expands significantly)
  • Isolation valves: Ball valves (manual) @ inlet and outlet per pump for service isolation
  • Pressure taps: Inlet and outlet (for monitoring); 1/8″ NPT standard
  • Temperature measurement: RTD pocket in discharge manifold (outlet)

7. Redundancy & Operational Modes

7.1 N+1 Configuration

  • Total units: 10 identical pumps (each ~15 kW motor)
  • Normal operation: 9 units active (demand: 50 g/s total); 1 standby (rotating duty, no failures expected in 24/7 mode)
  • Loss of 1 unit: 9 operating units maintain 50 g/s (slight overdrive; load redistributes automatically via common suction line)
  • Loss of 2 units: 8 operating units deliver ~44 g/s (90% capacity); slight pressure rise in coldbox but controllable
  • Isolation valves: Individual 24 VDC solenoid per pump; allows selective shutdown (e.g., unit #3 for maintenance)

7.2 Duty Cycling (Standby Rotation)

To extend MTBF and balance wear, standby unit rotates every 7 days:

  • Day 1–7: Units 1–9 active; Unit 10 idle
  • Day 8–14: Units 2–10 active; Unit 1 idle (solenoid opens Unit 1, closes Unit 2)
  • Cycle repeats every 70 days (all units share equivalent run hours)

8.2 Control Interface & Interlocks

  • ON/OFF command: 24 VDC logic (from site PLC); selectable via individual solenoid isolation valve per pump
  • Inlet starvation protection: If inlet pressure <0.05 barA, solenoid valve closes automatically (prevents cavitation damage)
  • Discharge overpressure: Solenoid-piloted relief opens @ 1.0 bar (vents to atmosphere)
  • Motor overtemp: If winding >130 °C, PLC receives signal; soft-stop (de-energises solenoid after 5 s delay)
  • Common suction manifold check valve: Prevents backflow from any pump to coldbox when inactive
