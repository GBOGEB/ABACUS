V3: RCW Heat Load (Oil Cooler)

Reference: B4 · E3

1
Given: CW heat rejection ALaT = 323.9 kW, inlet = 30°C, ΔT = 25°C, outlet = 55°C
Q = ṁ_cw × c_p × ΔT → ṁ_cw = Q / (c_p × ΔT)
2
Water properties: c_p ≈ 4.18 kJ/(kg·K)
ṁ_cw = 323.9 / (4.18 × 25) = 3.10 kg/s = 11.2 m³/h
3
Per unit (2 compressors):
ṁ_cw_per = 3.10 / 2 = 1.55 kg/s
Verdict
PASS — CW flow rate consistent with Q and ΔT. Max oil temp 85°C confirmed within Kaeser limits.
